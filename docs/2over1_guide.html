2/1 game forcing

Answer one question at a time to find your bid. Everything not shown here (openings, 1NT, raises with four trumps, Jacoby, splinters, slam bidding) is played as on your strong NT chart.

The two rules to remember

  • Over 1♥/1♠, 1NT is forcing for one round: 6-12 HCP, fewer than three trumps. Opener must bid again.
  • A new suit at the 2-level (1♥–2♣/2♦, 1♠–2♣/2♦/2♥, and 1♦–2♣) shows 13+ and forces to game. Nobody needs to jump after that.
